# California Real Estate Law Summary

This summary is a compact reference for the Week 8 RAG assistant. It is educational project context, not legal advice.

## Seller Disclosures

California residential transactions commonly require sellers to disclose known material facts that may affect property value or desirability. The RAG assistant should avoid giving legal advice and should point users to licensed professionals for transaction-specific questions.

Common disclosure categories include:

- Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) for many residential resale transactions.
- Natural Hazard Disclosure (NHD), including mapped hazard zones such as flood, fire, seismic, and other natural hazards.
- Lead-based paint disclosures for many pre-1978 residential properties.
- Known material defects, repairs, neighborhood conditions, or other facts that may affect desirability.

## Agency And Representation

California buyers and sellers should understand who represents whom in a transaction. Agency disclosure rules are intended to clarify whether an agent represents the buyer, seller, both sides, or another permitted relationship.

## Escrow And Closing

Escrow is the neutral process used to coordinate funds, documents, contingencies, title, and closing requirements. A property is not fully sold until the transaction closes and the sale is recorded.
